About AYiN

Operators, not observers.

We are advisors who open doors and stay until the channel is selling. No theory without traction, no strategy that doesn't end in a signed partner.

01  The name

AYiN means the eye.

In the languages of the ancient Near East, ayin is the eye — perception, insight, the faculty of seeing what others miss. It is the right name for what we do.

Entering the United States is rarely defeated by a weak product. It is defeated by misreading the market — the regional nuance, the buying culture, the partner who looks right but isn't. We are the eye that reads it clearly, before you commit.

Principles that hold
