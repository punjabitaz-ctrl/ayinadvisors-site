Our Practice

Four disciplines. One foothold.

Every engagement is built to move you from market curiosity to commercial traction — through the channel, not around it.

01

US Market Entry Research

Discovery

Before commitment comes clarity. We map the real shape of US demand for your category — where it concentrates, who already owns it, and the regulatory and pricing realities you'll meet on arrival. You get an honest read on whether, where and how to enter.

  • Market sizing & segmentation
  • Competitive & pricing landscape
  • Regulatory & compliance scan
  • Beachhead market recommendation
  • Demand & buyer-persona mapping
  • Go / no-go entry thesis
02

Channel Strategy & Enablement

Architecture

The route to market is the strategy. We design the channel architecture suited to your product and margins — distributors, resellers, agents or hybrid — then build the commercial terms, materials and training that turn a partner signature into actual sales.

  • Channel model & coverage design
  • Partner economics & incentives
  • Sales enablement toolkit
  • Onboarding & training program
  • Pricing & margin structure
  • Performance & KPI framework
03

B2B Partner Introductions

Access

Relationships open the US market — and we have them. We make warm, qualified introductions to the buyers, channel partners and decision-makers who actually move your category, then support the first conversations so they convert into agreements.

  • Target partner long-list & vetting
  • Warm, personally-made introductions
  • Meeting facilitation & prep
  • Deal & term negotiation support
  • Buyer & distributor outreach
  • Relationship handover
04

Regional Expansion · Canada & LATAM

Scale

Once the US engine runs, the continent opens up. We extend your proven playbook into Canada and Latin America — adapting channel models, partner profiles and commercial terms to each market's language, regulation and buying culture.

  • Canada market & channel entry
  • LATAM prioritization & sequencing
  • Localized partner sourcing
  • Cross-border commercial terms
  • Regulatory & tax navigation
  • Multi-market governance
