# Accessibility review: Phase 2b (accents and motion)

Date: 2026-10-21. Scope: all seven pages. Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA. Method: the
automated gate (axe-core in Chromium at 320, 768 and 1280px, static lint, pixel
contrast, behaviour and motion tests) plus Chromium emulation. Results below are
observations from this run; items marked "not performed" were not exercised by a
person.

## What this phase added

Decorative accents only: a letters band on Home, ripple rings, iris markers on
section heads, iris icons on the Home cards and steps, assembling cards, and a
pointer tilt on card grids (the tilt and the 3D depth were removed 2026-10-22 on owner request; cards and
glyphs now only fade and rise 24px). No visible copy was reworded, no link changed and
no form field changed; forms, Turnstile and the hero canvases are untouched.
Small markup changes that did happen: the section numbers (01 to 03 on Home and
About) and the Home card numbers (01 to 04) were replaced by iris markers and
icons, the Home process steps lost their separate gold dot (the step iris is
now the single marker on the progress line), and ids `approach` (About) and
`lt-latin` (Home letters band) were added so review entries have stable
selectors. All decorative elements are
`aria-hidden="true"` and not focusable. The one meaningful element, the letters
band, is a single `role="img"` with a text label ("... it means the eye").

Rules every placement follows: transforms and opacity only (plus the one-time
icon stroke draw); one easing; observers stop after the first run; the hidden
starting state exists only when `site.js` sets it, so a page whose script is
blocked shows all content. There is no pointer tilt or 3D depth (removed 2026-10-22 on owner request).

## Reduced motion

With the "reduce motion" preference on:

- `site.js` returns before registering assemble, drift and ripple
  emission, and the motion rules are inside a `no-preference` media query, so
  every element is in its final state.
- The existing global rule also stops every CSS animation and transition.
- Verified in Chromium with the preference emulated, on Home, About, Services,
  Projects and BizCheck: no element inside `main` has opacity below 1 or a
  non-identity transform (two fixed decorative opacities, the About eye ring and
  the CTA glow, are static and never animate), no element is in an
  emitting state, and no section is left in its hidden state. Tests:
  `test_reduced_motion_shows_final_state` and
  `test_rollout_reduced_motion_shows_final_state`.
- Also verified: content is fully visible with JavaScript disabled and with
  `site.js` blocked, and when the intersection observer never fires.

## WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide) and the 5 second cap

2.2.2 applies to moving content that starts automatically, lasts more than five
seconds and is presented alongside other content. This phase keeps every new
effect under that threshold:

- Ripples emit for two cycles of 2 s with a stagger, which ends before 5 s
  (the emission class is also removed at 5 s), then rest as static rings.
- Assemble transitions finish in under 3.5 s and run once per section.
- Drift responds to scroll and stops when the user stops; they are not time-based loops.
- Nothing flashes.
- Tests: `test_ripples_emit_then_rest_within_five_seconds`,
  `test_no_long_running_animation_after_load` (Home) and
  `test_rollout_no_long_running_animation` (other pages) sample
  `document.getAnimations()` six seconds after the placements were seen and
  require nothing of ours still running.

## Known pre-existing loops (not introduced here; Phase 3 must add a pause control)

These run longer than five seconds without a user-operable pause, so they do not
meet 2.2.2 and are recorded for Phase 3:

- Home: the `nodePulse` animation on the Why-section globe (infinite, starts
  when that band scrolls into view). The hero canvas and the scroll-hint dot
  also loop.
- Services and Projects: the header motif rotation (infinite, 60 s per turn).
- About: the eye-mark scan line (infinite, 14 s per turn).
- Projects: the opacity pulse on the "In Testing" status dot (infinite).
- BizCheck and Contact: the hero canvas animation.

The tests name these explicitly (the Home test lists `scrolldot`, `nodePulse`
and the finite `drawArc`; the other-page test excludes only the Projects status
pulse) so a new long-running animation cannot hide among them. All of them are
already stopped by the reduced-motion preference.

## Other observations

- Layout: no horizontal scroll at 320px on any page, including while sections
  assemble (tests step through each page at 320px).
- Axe: zero violations at 320, 768 and 1280px on all pages.
- Axe gate coverage: the gate now runs with the reduced-motion preference on,
  which renders every assemble item in its settled state. Under default
  motion, text still in its hidden starting state was opacity 0 and axe skipped
  it as hidden (46 text nodes on Home alone, plus the Services deliverable cells
  and the BizCheck steps). A test asserts the gate evaluates the letters-band
  captions and the Home card paragraphs.
- Axe "needs review": this phase added 18 colour-contrast entries to the review
  list (8 for About and BizCheck, 10 for Home) and retargeted one existing About
  entry whose selector changed. All come from the ripple rings: axe cannot
  attribute one background colour to text that has the decorative ripples SVG
  (aria-hidden, thin rings at 8 to 55% stroke opacity, no text) behind it. Most
  sit on the section heads they decorate (eyebrow, heading, intro paragraph of
  About "How we work", BizCheck "The Evaluators", Home "What we do" and "How it
  works"). Five have no rings behind them (two About stat-band captions, three
  Home letters-band items) and are paired with the next section's ripples box
  only by axe's coarse overlap grid; removing that box makes them disappear. The
  ripples boxes first reached into neighbouring sections; they now stay inside
  their own section, which removed many further entries. Every entry names
  its element and is backed by the rendered-pixel contrast test, which measures
  the text over the rings at 320, 768 and 1280px.
- Ripples on Services and Projects were removed: those pages have no section
  heading with room around it, and the rings read as faint arcs near text.
- Section-head rings (all four groups) do not drift with scroll, so their box
  never leaves the section; the drift option remains in the component but is
  unused on the pages.
- Print: assemble items print in their settled state, and the rings
  do nothing (a test emulates print media on Home, Services, Projects and
  BizCheck).
- The shared section-head layout (heading left, short intro right) now lives in
  the site stylesheet, so About matches Home; it previously stacked the intro
  tight under the heading because the rules were in Home's page styles only.
- Card grids on Services, Projects and BizCheck draw their cell borders on the
  cells instead of using a 1px grid gap over a line-coloured background, so
  hidden cards leave no coloured block behind them. A card is not promoted to its
  own compositing layer: a resting layer at a fractional
  offset made the 1px borders disappear or shift. A rendered-pixel test checks
  that every card keeps its right and bottom lines at 1280 and 768px.

## Not checked by a person

- A real screen reader (decorative accents are `aria-hidden`; nothing was
  listened to with NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver or TalkBack, including the letters band
  label).
- Real-device touch (the pointer-type check went away with the tilt; no phone or tablet was used).
- The operating-system reduced-motion setting (the preference was emulated in
  Chromium only).
- Real browsers other than Chromium, including font fallback for the Phoenician
  and Arabic glyphs on devices without those fonts.
- Forced-colours mode for the new accents.
