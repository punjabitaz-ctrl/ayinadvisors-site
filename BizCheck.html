03  Sample output

What you receive.

A structured score from each panelist, their top concerns, and a clear aggregate verdict — not a list of platitudes.

The three possible outcomes

GO
Clear to proceed.

The panel sees credible demand, a workable model and acceptable risk. A strong signal to invest the next phase of effort.

CONDITIONAL GO
Proceed — but fix these first.

The idea has real merit, but specific gaps in model, channel or execution must be addressed before committing capital.

NO GO
Don't proceed as defined.

The panel has identified structural obstacles — market, model, or competition — that make the current approach unlikely to succeed.
